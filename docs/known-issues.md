## Recycle bin: a restored shot is off its timelines and days (epic #90)

Binning a shot removes its clips from timelines (with the clips' own overlays) and its places on shooting days at once, because the Timeline and Plan save a cut or a day whole and would drop hidden rows on their next save anyway. A restore brings the shot, its folder placement and its desktop shortcuts back, not those clips and places. The Studio asks before binning a shot that is used there.

## Task view shows wireframes, not live pictures (epic #90)

The task view draws each workspace's windows as outlined boxes with the app's icon, and each window as a card with its title and icon. A live screenshot of every window would need rendering each app twice; the boxes keep the layout recognisable without it.

## Vignetting is a rough indication, and a native dependency (issue #266)

A rig's vignetting (Library › Rigs) is three numbers per focal length, interpolated between them: it ignores the aperture and the real falloff curve of the lens, so the darkened corners in the viewfinder and on photos are a hint of what the rig will show, not a measurement. On the phone the layer is an SVG radial gradient (`react-native-svg`, a native module): an installed app needs a new dev build or APK before it shows. A step of a sequence shows the vignette of its framing (or of the frame its overlay was drawn for).

## The Bluetooth plugin's location permissions are removed (lights #208)

`@sfourdrinier/react-native-ble-plx` with `neverForLocation` declares the location permissions a second time, capped at Android 11. Google Play rejects a permission declared twice with different `maxSdkVersion`s, so `withUncappedLocation` in `apps/mobile/app.config.ts` removes the capped copies; the app keeps them uncapped for GPS. A plugin update that declares them another way would bring the rejection back: Build phone app now fails when an upload is rejected (it used to pass, because the step piped into `tee`).

## Layers need the Studio to reach the phone (epic #275)

The phone shows a step's layers only through its bake, and only the Studio makes bakes (it draws the stack in the browser; a Worker has no canvas). The Studio's bakery bakes every stale step of the loaded shots in the background whenever it is open. Until then the phone shows the photo with "Layers not baked yet". Right after migration 0030, every shot that had overlays has stale steps: the first Studio session bakes them (a few seconds per step, one shot at a time). A server-side baker would remove this dependency; it is not built.

## What a bake leaves out (epic #275)

A bake is photo-sized, so the phone frames it like the photo in every view mode. Anything beyond the photo is not in it: layers below the footage (they only show in the area beyond the capture) and shapes drawn outside the picture. A timeline clip's own layers are Studio-only (the phone never reads timelines). A clip re-framed differently from its step shows its strip thumbnail from the step's bake (frame-space layers at the step's frame); the stage and the clip panel compose the clip's own frame live.

## Old overlays are kept, unread (epic #275)

Migration 0030 copied the overlays into layers and left the `overlays` table, its renders under `renders/overlays/` and `shot_steps.overlay_id` in place. Nothing reads them any more (the purge still deletes their renders with a shot). A later release drops them once the migration has proven itself on production.

## Live picture: what is not tested yet (issue #302)

The picture works on the owner's phone (docs/features/live-picture.md). Not tried yet:
- a glass-to-glass latency measurement against HollyView;
- the transmitter's limit on viewers (HollyView and OpenClapper at the same time);
- other phones' decoders;
- Wi-Fi drops mid-stream.

The dev client loads its JavaScript from Metro, which the transmitter's Wi-Fi cannot reach: open the app on another network first, then switch.

## The dev client on Google Play is a release build (deploy#22)

Play rejects debuggable bundles, so "OpenClapper Dev" from the internal track is a release build that keeps expo-dev-client's launcher and menu (`expo.devlauncher.configureInRelease`, `expo.devmenu.configureInRelease`, set by `app.config.ts`). The switch does not bring expo-dev-launcher's debug manifest along (SDK 57), so the app's manifest declares the launcher's activities itself (`withDevClientInRelease`); the first Play build without them crashed on start. React Native's dev support follows `ReactBuildConfig.DEBUG` (off in a release build), so the plugin also passes `useDevSupport = true` in `MainApplication` and allows cleartext HTTP; without that the launcher could not load from Metro. After an SDK upgrade, compare them with `expo-dev-launcher/android/src/debug/AndroidManifest.xml`, and check that a Play build still starts and loads from Metro. Checked on the owner's phone 2026-11-12 (version code 3611038): the launcher, the tunnel URL from `make mobile`, LogBox, reload, sign-in and a capture work. Open: on the first start of a fresh install, right after the camera permission was granted, LogBox showed `Uncaught (in promise): … Timeouted: JPromise was destroyed!` once; after a reload it did not come back. Whether the debug build does the same on a fresh install is not checked. `make dev-build` (OpenClapper Dev Local, a debug build) stays the fallback.
