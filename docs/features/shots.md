# Shots, sets and locations

> **Built in step B6 ([#378](https://github.com/OpenClapper-dev/core/issues/378)).** Specification: [04 shots](../rewrite/04-shots.md), [09a places](../rewrite/09a-places.md), [I1 interjection](../rewrite/i1-interjection.md), [U3 shot](../rewrite/ux-3-shot.md). Replaces the first app's shots that owned photos, steps, layers and bakes, the review state, locations with spots and pins, and GPS on photos (review-and-tagging, composition, positions pages).

## The files (`packages/model/src/shot.ts`, `types.ts`)

| Type | What it is | References | Body |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `shot` | An intent: one planned picture of the film | `content` (any files, in order), `item` (the progression's pictures, in order), `overlay` (at most one) | `{ progression: null \| { timing, frame, overlayShown } }` (version 2; a shot saved as `{}` reads as no progression) |
| `set` | The place in the story where a scene plays (Motiv) | `content` | `{}` |
| `location` | The real place where it is filmed (Drehort) | `content` | `{}` |

- **Content** is an ordered list of references to files of any type of the project. Nothing is stored per entry; one file can be in any number of lists; a removed file leaves its entry, shown as missing.
- **The progression** (shots only, at most one) is a timed order of pictures. An `item` reference points at a photo or a canvas (the Worker refuses another type with `400`; a missing one is kept) and carries `{ ms, frame?, move? }`: its own time (100 ms to 10 min), for a canvas the frame it shows, and for a smooth move the frame it moves to and whether it eases (`move: { to, ease }`). The `overlay` reference is a canvas drawn over every item.
- **Timing**: `each` (every item its own `ms`), `even` (one `total` split evenly) or `ends` (one `total`, the first and the last held twice as long); `itemTimes` computes it.
- **Default frame**: frame values (`FrameValues`: from a preset with a focal length, free-form, or an aspect ratio; the rules of a frame on a canvas). Applied, centred, to items with an angle of view and no frame of their own. A default frame from a preset keeps that lens in use: the Worker writes it into `frames` as frame `default` of the shot.
- **The cover** (thumbnail): the first item of the progression, else the first photo or canvas in the content, else a placeholder (`shotCover`).
- **Internal files**: a canvas made inside a shot (the generator's canvas, the overlay, a new canvas made there) is created with `internal_of` set to the shot: hidden from the browser and search, binned, restored and copied with the shot, and released one way with `POST /api/files/:id/release` ([files](files.md)).
- **Places properties are added where they are needed** (#426: a new project starts without properties): the map offers "Add a Position property" (on locations), a set's "Shots that play here" offers "Add a Set property" (on shots, one set), a location's "Sets filmed here" offers "Add a Locations property" (on sets, any number). Ordinary definitions: change, hide or delete them; any reference or position property of the project works the same.

## The Shot, Set and Location windows (`apps/studio/src/apps/ShotApp.tsx`, `src/shots/`)

One window per file, opened from any browser (double-click) or a chip. The default layout of U3: the **stage** with the chosen picture, **Properties** at the right, **Progression** (shots only) and **Content** below; panels are moved, sized, collapsed or stacked as tabs like everywhere (`Dock`), "Panels back to their places" resets them.

- **The stage** shows the chosen entry: a photo, or a canvas (its render; one never saved shows its base photo with its frames), through its frame in Outline, Mask, Crop (default) or Full. Beyond the photo is hatched. A caption says which frame and where it came from (its own, the default frame, the canvas's picture). **Open** opens the file's own app; **Edit here** (a canvas, or a double-click) mounts the canvas editor in place of the stage and the side panel, with a bar naming the file and whether it is internal; **Draw or frame** on a photo makes an internal canvas on it, puts it after the photo and edits it here. An empty file offers Add files and Draw a canvas. A missing entry offers to restore the file (from the bin) or to remove the entry.
- **The side panel** has two tabs: the file of the window (cover with the rule that chose it, name, the project's properties, for a set the shots that point at it, for a location the sets (with no reference property for that yet, a button adds "Set" or "Locations"), its internal files with what they are used for, Edit here, Make it a project file, Delete when unused, "Used in", System) and the chosen entry (its place in the progression: time, Ease and a slider to look through a smooth move, which frame of a canvas it shows, where its frame comes from; then its own facts and properties).
- **Content**: cards or rows, reordered by dragging; "Add files" opens a picker over the project (search, a type filter; a collection opens and its photos are ticked one by one or all; nothing internal); "New canvas" makes an internal canvas. A card's menu: Edit here, Open, Draw or frame, Add to the progression, Make it a project file, Remove (an internal file is deleted with its entry once nothing of the shot points at it).
- **Progression** (shots): a strip where each picture is as wide as it holds (80 px a second); click to show, drag to reorder, pull the right edge to change its time (in "each its own time"), × removes; the first carries the thumbnail star; a smooth move says so. **Play** runs it on the stage on the clock, with a playhead. **Timing**: each its own time, one length split evenly, or one length with the first and last held twice as long. **Default frame**: none, a preset with a focal length, free-form values, or an aspect ratio with its size. **Overlay**: "Add an overlay" makes an internal canvas on transparent paper, edited here; a switch shows it over every picture, stretched to the picture's frame. **Generate…** (also "Generate a zoom…" without a progression) and "From pictures…" / "Add pictures" (photos, canvases, or one frame of a canvas). The menu puts its files into the content too, or removes the progression (its internal files with it).
- **The generator** makes zooms only, from a photo with an angle of view and a preset or free-form values (format, multiplier, squeeze, delivery). In steps: the ticked primes of a preset, or for a zoom preset and free values common stops, every so many millimetres, or a typed list (whole mm), between From and To (their order is the direction); each picture holds a time. Smooth: from one focal length to another over a time, eased or not; one moving picture. At most 60 frames. The preview shows every frame on the photo and says which see more than it. The result is one internal canvas with a frame per focal length on the photo (named "Framing series" or "Zoom", or as typed) and a picture per frame; it replaces the progression or is added to its end.
- **The edit lock** (shots; sets and locations have none): one window edits a shot, the one that opened it first; others, the Shot window mounted in a timeline included, see it read-only (what changes it muted) with who edits it, Edit when it is free, and Stop editing for the editor. Watchers follow every change at once in a project set to realtime, else read it again every 5 s. Property values are not under the lock. On the phone a content change to a shot someone edits is refused with a message.
- **Saving**: every change shows at once and is saved 0.4 s later as one `PUT /api/files/:id` of the references (and a shot's body); the Studio's file store reads the project again after. The store holds internal files too (`GET /api/files?internal=1`); every list leaves them out.
- **Lists**: a shot's, set's or location's card in a browser shows its cover through its frame; rows show the cover's own thumbnail. A canvas never saved in the editor shows its base photo.
- **Transparent paper**: a blank canvas can have no colour (`surface.color: null`, "Transparent" in the surface's row; a checkerboard in the editor); its render is a PNG with transparency. Overlays start that way.

## Places: Sets, Locations and the map (`apps/studio/src/places/`)

- **Sets** and **Locations** are entries on the file browser, as Shots is: the browser set to that type, flat, with its properties as columns, "New set" / "New location" (named by the pattern), filters in the sidebar, the board. Files makes them too (New, Set / Location). A double-click opens the Set or Location window (above).
- **The map view** is a fourth view of the browser, next to list, grid and board, offered where it can show something: in Locations always (its first view), elsewhere once a listed file has a position. One pin per file with a position (the value of its first position property), in its type's colour; a click makes it current, its popup opens it; the view fits the pins once, and a note says how many have none. In a project without a position property the note offers "Add a Position property" instead (on locations). Leaflet 1.9.4 with OpenStreetMap's tiles (attribution, and a Referer of the origin as the tile server asks); in the dark theme the tiles are darkened. Nothing is looked up: an address is never sent anywhere.
- **The pin editor**: a position property has a pin button beside its text field (which takes "lat, lng" or a map link): a map where a click places the pin and a drag moves it; Keep saves it, "Remove the pin" clears it.
- **References open files**: a reference value's chip opens the file (its app's window); its cross takes it out of the value.

## On the phone and tablet (`apps/mobile/src/apps/Places.tsx`, `src/places/`)

- **Shots, Sets and Locations** are apps with a touch form, so each can be a tab (Setup, Tabs on this device; a new device has Shots). Each lists its files as tiles with their cover, cropped to its frame like the Studio's Crop; the plus makes a new one (named by the pattern; needs a connection). Locations opens on a **map** (Leaflet 1.9.4 and OpenStreetMap's tiles in a web view, as the first app did; needs a connection) with a pin per location with a position and a note of how many have none (without a position property it says to add one in the Studio, whose map offers it; the phone does not make property definitions); a tap opens it. List shows them as tiles.
- **The touch form**, one for all three: the chosen picture (cropped to its frame, the progression's default frame for photos, the overlay over a shot's pictures); for a shot with a progression, **Play** (on the clock; a smooth move plays as the frame between its two frames) and its pictures as a strip; the name (saved when the field is left); the **content** as a strip of pictures (tap to show; press and hold: Draw on it for a canvas, Earlier, Later, Remove from the content), **Add files** (photos, canvases, shots, sets, locations of the project) and **Draw a canvas** (a new internal canvas in the content, opened in the editor); for a set the shots that play there and for a location the sets filmed there (a tap opens them in their tab); then the properties, the same controls as for a photo in Media. Changes to the content and the name need a connection; property values wait on the phone until they can be sent, as for photos.
- **What the phone loads**: with Media's library, the shots, sets and locations of the project with their references, bodies and values, and the canvases made inside shots (`internal=1`); a canvas's frames are read from the server when one of its pictures is shown.
- The progression is played, not edited, on the phone (ux-5); the Studio edits it.
