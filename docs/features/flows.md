# Flows

The Flows extension ([extensions](extensions.md)), rebuild step B10 ([#382](https://github.com/OpenClapper-dev/core/issues/382)); it needs [Hardware](hardware.md). The model is [08 · Extensions](../rewrite/08-extensions.md) ("The Flows extension"), the design [U6](../rewrite/ux-6-devices.md). A **flow** is a timed programme that drives what devices expose: the first app's light sequences ([lights](lights.md), "Sequences"), carried over whole, and grown to cameras.

## A flow (`packages/ext-flows/src/flow.ts`)

A **file of a project** (type `flow`), edited by one person at a time like a timeline (the edit lock; the others watch it live or every 5 s, per the project's setting). Its body (body version 2): `{ durationMs, loop, tracks, markers, stages? }`; its name is the file's. Version 1 had one optional `stage`; it is upgraded to `stages: [{ id: "s1", name: "Stage 1", … }]` (`stagesFromV1`, the file type's `upgrade`): by the server on the next save, and by every client reading an older body (`flowBodyOf`; the phone also upgrades the flows and unsent edits it keeps).

| Part | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Track | `{ id, name, model, device, muted?, color?, locked?, keyframes }`: of one catalogue model (a light: Aputure MC, NEEWER FS230B, FS300B; or the Blackmagic camera), driving one device of the account once assigned (an unassigned track does not play), one of 8 colours |
| Start keyframe | Every track has one at 0 that cannot be moved or deleted. A light's sets power, intensity and white or colour; a camera's stops recording. A flow never starts from what a device happened to do |
| Locks | A light track may **lock** parameters on its start keyframe (#426; `locked`: `power`, `intensity`, `kelvin`, `gm`, `colour` (hue and saturation as one), `effect`, `curve`, the ids of the catalogue's light controls): the start's value then holds for the whole track. Other keyframes' values for a locked parameter are **kept but ignored** (`playedKeyframes`: a keyframe that sets it plays the start's value, or none when the start leaves it unset; so a switch between white and colour still happens), and come back when it is unlocked. The player, the graph, the light colour and the value labels all use what plays. Checked on save: known parameters, each once, none on a camera track |
| Light keyframe | Holds any of power, intensity, white (K, G/M), colour (hue, saturation), an effect with its options, the dimming curve; unset ones keep what came before. Between two keyframes the light keeps the first's values and jumps at the second, unless the second has a **fade in** (a curve: linear, exp, log, S): then everything that differs blends over the gap (hue the short way; white ↔ colour switches at the end). `fadeStepped`: on a light that fades by itself, send the fade in 0.1 s steps instead; kept but ignored while native fades are off (#426) |
| Camera keyframe | `controls`: values of the camera's catalogue controls that hold a value (record, ISO, shutter, white balance, the lens, the image, audio, slate, monitoring levels…), by control id. They jump; no fades |
| Margins | Any keyframe (#426) may have a **margin before** and **after** (`marginBeforeMs`, `marginAfterMs`: half seconds, up to the flow's length; the start keyframe only after): the least distance to the keyframe next to it on that side of the track; between two keyframes the larger of the margins facing each other counts. "Minimum gap, pushes" (`moveKeyframes` in `edit.ts`): moving a keyframe (a drag, a new time in the panel, a selection, a new margin, a keyframe added inside one) pushes a neighbour that would come inside a margin, and the push goes on through the keyframes after it (a pushed one keeps at least half a second from the one pushing it); those further away stay put. Nothing is pushed off the flow or onto the start keyframe, which never moves: a move that does not fit is cut short half a second at a time (a selection together), a margin that cannot fit is refused. Keyframes without a margin between them may still pass each other, never share a time |
| Marker | A named point or range, across the flow or on one track; playback ignores them |
| Stages | Optional (#426, `stage.ts`): up to 12 stages, each `{ id, name, aspect, objects, lights, underlay?, turn? }`, a drawing tied to the flow (below, "The stage"); several stages are several **perspectives** on the same tracks (a floor plan, a photo of the set with the lights where they stand, one light from another side). `id` 1 to 40 letters, digits, `-` or `_`, unique in the flow; `name` 1 to 60 characters. `objects` are canvas objects of the kinds pen, line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse, text and stencil (the canvas's own rules, fractions of the paper; no placed photos, no groups), at most 500 with 40,000 points in all; `lights` one widget per placed **light** track `{ track, x, y, rot, mount? }` (fractions of the paper, degrees; `mount` "aimed", the default when absent, or "ceiling", shown as Top-down), a track at most once per stage and on any number of stages; `underlay` a photo of the project under the drawing with an opacity; `turn` how the whole paper is turned on screen, whole degrees 0 to 359 (absent: 0) |

Times snap to half seconds (`SNAP_MS` = 500 ms, #426; shown as "12 s" or "12.5 s"; a keyframe or marker saved off that grid before keeps its time until it is moved). Limits: up to 1 hour, 64 tracks, 2,000 keyframes, 200 markers, 12 stages of 500 drawn objects each (and the body's size limit). Checked on every save (`flowError`, the stages with `stagesError` and `stageError`).

## The stage (`packages/ext-flows/src/stage.ts`)

Feedback round 8 of #426, the prototype `flow-stage.html`: a drawing board inside a flow (a top-down plan of a tunnel, say) where a light track is placed as a **widget**; pressing Play shows the sequence on it, without hardware. Part of the flow, not a file: the same edit lock, save and live room. A flow may have several stages (#426 feedback: "different perspectives, not different stages"): each its own drawing, underlay, aspect and placements, all showing the same tracks at the same playhead. A flow without stages shows an empty "Stage 1", stored with the first thing drawn or placed on it. Its paper is **dark in both themes**, as content (like a photo), so light reads; colours on it are content colours, not theme tokens.

- **A widget shows what the track plays** at the playhead (`widgetState`): the track's keyframes as they play (locks applied, `playedKeyframes`) sampled with `sampleTrack`, as the player does. Off or on; intensity as the beam's length, width and brightness; white (kelvin) or colour as its tint (`lightColour`); an effect as one of four **motions** (pulse, strobe, flicker, flash: `EFFECT_FAMILY`, `effectOpacity`) with the effect's name on its tag; a muted track dark and dashed. A track needs no device to show on the stage.
- **Its shape follows the fixture** (`widgetShape`): the Aputure MC a small panel with an RGB face and a soft, wide beam; a NEEWER FS a COB head with a reflector and a narrower, longer beam, the FS300B larger. Shapes, beams, tag places and motions are shared, so the Studio and the device draw the same.
- **Mounted: Aimed or Top-down** (`mount`, `ceilingShape`, `poolRadius`; #426 feedback): an **aimed** light (the default; on a stand or a wall) is drawn as above, seen from the side with a beam across the floor and a direction. A **top-down** light points straight down (from a ceiling, a boom or a stand; stored as `mount: "ceiling"`): its body seen from below (the MC a square panel, an FS a round COB in its reflector) and its light a **round pool** on the floor around it, wider and brighter with the intensity, in its colour, with the same effect motions; off it is a dark body without a pool. It has no direction: its turn is kept (switched back, it points where it did) but not drawn, and its tag sits under it (above it at the paper's foot). "Aimed" rather than "wall", as most such lights stand on a stand; aimed is stored as no `mount` at all.
- **Lights only**: a camera track is never placed. Removing a track removes its widget from every stage (`removeTrack`).
- **Underlay**: a photo of the project under the drawing, fitted inside the paper (the drawing keeps its shape), with an opacity, like the canvas's base photo. It is kept by id in the body, without a reference: a photo deleted later just no longer shows; a copy of the project's files points at the copied photo (`FLOW.remap`). The canvas has no map surface, so there is no map underlay.

## Playing (`packages/ext-flows/src/player.ts`)

One player for the Studio and the phone, so a flow plays the same wherever it runs: every 100 ms it samples each assigned, unmuted track and sends only what changed. Every fade goes in those 0.1 s steps, on every light. **Native fades are off** (#426, `NATIVE_FADES` in `flow.ts`): the MC can run a fade tile by itself (one `fade` command per tile, chained over 25.5 s), but on the owner's lights that was unreliable and started oddly; the code stays, the player sends no `fade` while the switch is off, and the editors offer no choice of how a fade runs. Lights sharing one connection (MCs on one mesh) take turns on it, so with N of them fading at once each gets a step about every N × 0.12 s ([lights](lights.md), `queue.ts`). A light with switchable smoothing (NEEWER) jumps at keyframes and smooths inside fades. A camera track sends a control when its held value changes. Loop restarts at 0 and sends everything again.

## Running on the device (channel `flows`, `packages/ext-flows/src/protocol.ts`)

A client saves the flow and names the revision it saved (`updated_at`): `run { flow, revision, fromMs }`; the proxy phone fetches exactly that revision (asked again a few times if the server still has the previous one) and plays it on its own clock through its own connections. `stop`, `hello`. It reports `state { phase: idle | loading | playing | error, flow, name, revision, atMs, durationMs, loop, error }` on every change, every 250 ms while playing, and on `hello`.

## In the Studio (`apps/studio/src/ext/flows/`)

The app **Flows** (one window for the project's flows): the list of flows in a panel, with the chosen one's name (saved once typing settles, about 0.7 s, or on Enter or leaving the field; not on every key), length, loop, Duplicate and Delete under it; the tracks in the middle; what is chosen in the other panel. The panels are the Dock's: move, size, collapse, stack as tabs. **Edit** / **Stop editing** as on a timeline; while someone else edits, it is read-only and follows their changes.

- **Tracks**: Add a track picks a light model or the camera; a track's device is picked on its label (only devices of that model, one track per device; a device that is gone shows so). The bulb **mutes** a track (left out of playback and Live); the palette sets its colour (8, or none); "…" shows the track in the panel (rename, back to the common height, remove). The grip at the left **reorders** tracks by dragging; the lower edge of a label makes that track taller or lower.
- **Keyframes**: two modes, **Select** (V, default: a click on empty lane space clears) and **Insert** (I: a click adds a keyframe); **Alt+click** adds in Select too. Shift-click adds to or removes from the selection; a box drawn over the lanes selects across tracks; dragging a selected keyframe moves the whole selection (half seconds; start keyframes stay; pushing neighbours out of margins, which come back when the drag does); with the intensity graph shown, dragging a light keyframe up or down also sets its intensity, snapped to the bar's **Snap**. Delete removes, Mod+A selects all, Esc clears. A keyframe's **margins** show as hatched bands before and after it; in Select mode the keyframe pointed at or chosen shows **trim handles** at their edges (beside it while it has none) to drag a margin in half seconds, to 0 to remove it. A light keyframe's panel sets its time and margins (before, after), power, intensity, white or colour (hue and saturation beside a large swatch of the colour they make, drawn as the lanes draw it: `hsiCss`, the hue mixed with white; a click on the swatch opens a **colour field** to pick it by eye, hue across and saturation up, the sliders following), an effect with its options, the dimming curve (each checked: set here; unchecked: as before). The start keyframe's panel has a **Lock** (a padlock, closed while locked) beside each value; on the other keyframes a locked value is not shown, a note names what is locked, and a locked intensity is not dragged up or down (#426). A camera keyframe's panel sets its time and margins and checks the camera controls it sets (record, ISO, shutter…).
- **Fades**: a click on the strip between two keyframes makes it a fade and shows it: Jump or Fade and the curve (Linear, Expo, Log, S-curve). While native fades were on (before #426), the MC also had whether the light runs it or it goes in steps (a dashed tile); hidden now.
- **Markers**: M at the playhead, Shift+M twice for a range, or in Insert mode (or with Alt) a click (point) or drag (range) in the marker strip under the ruler or a lane's top strip. They select, move and delete like keyframes; a range's edges drag; a marker's panel sets name, in, out, range or point, and scope.
- **View** (kept per browser): intensity graph, light colour, value labels, effect and power badges; keyframe size S, M, L. In the panel **View** (#426: a dock panel of its own, moved, stacked and collapsed like the others; as big as its content until its border is dragged, a double-click on the border fits it again; at a side its boxes stack, below they stand in a row; it holds View and Customize, **Stage**, Snap and Size, so the bar keeps only the modes, Undo / Redo, Live, the time and playing): **Snap** 1 %, 5 % or 10 % (kept per browser, default 1 %): an intensity drag stops on that grid, and from a level off it (typed in the panel, where any percent stays valid) first at the next line in the drag's direction. **Size** is two grouped boxes: the timeline's width (a slider from fit to 32× and **Fit width**; the lanes then scroll sideways with the track labels kept in place) and one height for every track (a slider and **Fit height**, which shares the room). No help text under the lanes and no Marker button.
- **Customize** (a popover in the View panel, #426; kept with the account in the preferences file, so it applies in the Studio and on every phone and tablet): per light model (the models of this flow first) which **parameters** show at all (`lightParamsHidden`; default all): a hidden one disappears from every keyframe panel, the start keyframe, its lock, and the device cards and panels of Hardware (`controlGroups`; hiding the effect hides Strike now too); and which **fields** every keyframe panel shows (`keyframeFieldsHidden`: At, Margin before, Margin after, each parameter; a light or camera keyframe alike). Both only hide: stored values, locks and what plays do not change. The two are independent of each other and of locks. Not in Settings (no extension hook there yet).
- **The stage** (`stage.tsx`, #426): above the lanes, with a divider dragged to share the room (its height and whether it shows are kept per browser; **Stage** in the View panel hides it, which is the editor without it). Its bar starts with the **stage picker**: the shown stage's name (a list of the flow's stages once there are two or more; which one is shown is kept per browser and flow, with the stage layout) and a menu: **New stage** (empty, named "Stage N"), **Rename…**, **Duplicate** (its drawing, underlay and placements, "… copy", after it), **Delete…** (asks first; the tracks stay). New and Duplicate stop at 12. Switching is allowed read-only; the menu needs the edit. Then come the canvas's tools (Move V, Pen P, Line L, Arrow A, Rectangle R, Ellipse O, Text T, Stencil S with the canvas's stencils, Eraser E; drawn with the canvas's own object code, `canvas/objects.tsx`), colours that read on the dark paper, and **Underlay** (a project photo and its opacity). The panel **Place a light** lists the light tracks for the **shown stage** ("N placed" counts that stage; a track placed on another stage can be placed here too): drag one onto the paper, or **Place** (the middle); a placed one is shown on the stage with the crosshair; camera tracks are listed but cannot be placed. On the paper a widget is dragged to move it and, when aimed, turned with its handle (15° steps, Shift: free); clicking one chooses it: the panel **Chosen** shows its track, model, device, **Mounted** (Aimed / Top-down, undoable; a newly placed light is aimed), its turn (aimed only) and what it shows at the playhead (live while playing), with **Show track** and **Remove from the stage**; its track's label is marked in the lanes. Choosing a keyframe, a fade or a track's "…" in the lanes rings that track's widget. A drawn object chosen shows its colour, text, stencil and size, and on the paper the canvas's handles: its corners resize it, a line's or an arrow's two ends move (#426); moving, resizing, an end and a light **snap** (the crosshair in the bar, on by default; Ctrl or ⌘ held: not this time) within 6 px to the paper's edges and middle, the other drawn objects' edges and middles and the other lights, with a guide line, as on the canvas; a chosen object other than a line or an arrow also has the canvas's **rotate** handles outside its corners (Shift: steps of 15°; a turned object resizes about its own axes, without snapping); a **double-click** on a text edits it in place; **the view** (a box over the paper's corner): **turn** the whole stage 15° left or right (Shift: 90°; the degrees shown, a click on them turns it back to 0°): drawing, lights and underlay turn together like a sheet on a desk, saved with the stage (undoable, needs the edit) and shown the same on the device; **zoom** (−, the percent: fit, +; Ctrl or ⌘ with the wheel zooms at the pointer) and pan (the wheel, or a drag with the middle button) are this window's; Delete removes what is chosen on the stage; Alt while dragging a drawn object drags a copy of it, as on the canvas (a light is one per track, never copied). **Undo / Redo** (Mod+Z, Mod+Shift+Z; buttons in the bar) cover the tracks and the stages together (the list of stages too: new, renamed, duplicated, deleted); quick successive changes (a drag) are one step. Read-only (someone else edits) a widget can still be chosen to read its state.
- **Playing**: Space plays from the playhead and, again, stops and goes back to where play started; K stops where it is. Without a proxy, Play drives the **stage** alone (#426): the player runs, nothing is sent; with the connections held it also drives the lights. No banner says so: the "No proxy" pill in the title bar and Play's tooltip do. **Live** (on by default) sends the state at the playhead while stopped, and every edit; a camera's recording is never started or stopped by Live, only by playing. While a flow plays from here, its devices are locked in Devices. A proxy that appears again gets everything sent anew.
- **Run on device** (from the start, or the button beside it from the playhead): the editor saves first and the device plays exactly that revision; meanwhile the flow is locked, its playhead follows the phone, and Play and Live are off for every flow. Stop in the banner or Space stops it.

## On the device (phone or tablet) (`apps/mobile/src/ext/flows/`)

The app **Flows** (a tab like any other while the project has the extension on), with its own **native editor** (#426: no web view; the device that edits or runs a flow is the one connected to the lights, often without a connection to the server):

- **The project's flows** (`store.ts`): kept on the device (`flows.v1`), so they open, change and play offline. **New flow** works offline too. A row says when a change is not on the server yet; **Run** (online, saved flows) runs it on the device that holds the connections, as the Studio's Run on device.
- **Saving** (`store.ts`): every change is kept on the device at once (`flowEdits.v1`) and sent about a second later, or with the next sync. While online the editor holds the **edit lock** (renewed every 30 s) and saves under it; when someone else holds it, the flow is read-only on the device and shows their saves. Offline it edits freely. It also joins the file's live room: when the lock is let go or runs out it takes it at once, and while someone else edits, each of their saves is read at once. When the server's flow changed meanwhile, or was deleted, the device's version is saved **beside it as a copy** ("… (from this device)"): nothing made on the device is lost, nothing made elsewhere is overwritten. A change the server refuses stays on the device with the reason, and is tried again after the next change.
- **The editor** (`ui/Editor.tsx`, `ui/Lanes.tsx`, `ui/Inspect.tsx`; the editing rules shared with the Studio in `packages/ext-flows/src/edit.ts`): the ruler (tap: the playhead), the marker strip and one lane per track, with the track labels beside them. Under the bar, the same two **Size** boxes as in the Studio (width 1× to 32× with Fit; track height with Fit; each Fit is off while the size already is what it would set). **View** (an eye in the bar) has the Studio's switches: intensity graph (a light's keyframes sit at their level on the line, and dragging one up or down sets its intensity, shown while dragging and snapped like in the Studio), light colour (a dot filled with what the light shows there), value labels, effect and power badges; and the keyframe size, small for a pen, large for a finger; the intensity snap (1 %, 5 %, 10 %). All of it is kept on this device (`flows.view.v1`). The drawing rules are shared with the Studio (`packages/ext-flows/src/look.ts`). Three **modes** in the bar (#426): **Select** (a tap on a keyframe, fade or marker only chooses it, shown highlighted; keyframes and markers are dragged sideways to move them, a range keeps its length), **Insert** (a tap on a lane adds a keyframe with the intensity the track has there, a tap on the strip a marker; either is chosen, nothing opens; dragging works as in Select) and **Edit** (a tap on a keyframe, fade or marker opens its sheet; nothing is dragged). A tap on a track's label opens the track's sheet in every mode. Two toggles in the bar keep a keyframe drag to one axis, **Time** (↔) and **Intensity** (↕), filled while on: Time on, a drag moves only the time; Intensity on, only the intensity; one at a time, kept on the device. **Loop** (the repeat button beside Play) switches the flow's loop. Upright the bar takes two rows: Track / Stage, the modes and View spread over the first; the axis toggles at the left and the time, To the start, Loop and Play at the right of the second. On its side it is one row. The strip between two keyframes (a thick bar along the lane's foot) is the fade. Margins show as bands before and after a keyframe, stronger on the chosen one. The sheets (opened in Edit mode): a light's keyframe (its time, margin before and after in half seconds, power, intensity, white or colour with a large swatch beside hue and saturation (a tap opens the colour field under them), effect, dimming curve; on the start keyframe a **Lock** chip with a padlock beside each value, on the others locked values left out and named in a note, and a locked intensity not dragged), a camera's keyframe (time, margins, the catalogue controls), the fade (jump or fade, curve), a marker (name, where, a range), a track (name, the device it drives, muted, colour, delete). **Add a track** lists the light and camera models. The flow's name, length and loop are in its settings sheet. **Customize keyframes and lights** (in View) is the Studio's Customize, as switches, with the same account-wide preferences.
- **Track / Stage** (`ui/Stage.tsx`, #426): a switch at the start of the bar (kept on the device). **Stage** shows one of the flow's stages **read only**, picked above it when there are several (up to three as a switch of their names, more as a list in a sheet; kept on the device per flow): the drawing and the light widgets drawn natively (react-native-svg) from the same shared shapes, beams, tags and motions as the Studio's, an underlay photo (from the device's photo cache when it has it), the stencils as the design system's glyphs; nothing is drawn, placed or moved here. Top-down lights are drawn as pools, as in the Studio. A turned stage is shown turned, fitted into the room it has. A tap on a widget shows a card with how it is mounted and its state at the playhead (power, intensity, white or colour, effect) and **Show track** (back to Track view, the track's sheet open). Under it a **mini timeline**: the light tracks' keyframes as dots in their colours, the playhead's thumb; a tap or drag moves the playhead, also while playing. The bar keeps the time, To the start and Play / Pause. It works offline, as the stage is part of the flow's body.
- **Play and Live on this device**: the shared player through this device's own connections; **Hold the connections here** switches the proxy on when it is off. Without the connections held, Play drives the stage alone (nothing is sent; #426). Live (on by default) shows the flow at the playhead on the lights while stopped and follows every edit; recording on a camera starts only while playing.
- **The run** (`run.ts`, kept from the first app's `sequenceRun.ts`): the proxy phone fetches the named revision (asked again a few times while the server still has the previous one), plays it with the shared player through its own connections, reports its state, and keeps playing when the Studio closes. A banner shows it with **Stop**, here and in the Studio. It stops when the proxy lets go of its connections: the proxy switched off, or a pairing taking the radio.

## On the server

No routes of its own: a flow is a file (`/api/files`), its lock and live room are the core's.
